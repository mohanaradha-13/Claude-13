Who this run is built for

One self-driven person. No bootcamp, no cohort, no paid mentor. Everything below uses free materials — official docs, YouTube, blogs, and free Coursera audits — sequenced so you never wonder what to learn next.

// init weights — where you start
  • › You're 25+ and treat learning as a serious habit, not a hobby.
  • › Python feels approachable — you can write variables, loops, and simple functions.
  • › You've heard of machine learning and have a rough idea of what it does.
  • › You have a laptop, reliable Wi-Fi, and ~18 spare hours a week.
  • › You can stay disciplined without anyone checking on you.

Epoch 12 · independent

The final month is yours alone.

Eleven epochs of training come down to this: one full month to design, build, deploy, and document a complete AI project — by yourself. No guided steps, no checklist to copy. Just you, your toolbelt, and a problem worth solving.

Use Claude when you're stuck — to debug, to review, to think out loud. But the architecture, the decisions, and the build are yours. That's the difference between someone who finished a course and someone who can ship.
